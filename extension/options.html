Wallabagger for Sandstorm

In your wallabag grain, open Sandstorm’s key icon and create an API token with Library access. Paste the resulting webkey here. It grants full access to this library; you can revoke it in Sandstorm.

Use the library address from your browser’s address bar. Article links will open there.

To save, open an article and click Wallabagger, or press Alt+Shift+W. It sends the page content you can currently see, including articles you are signed in to read. Linked images are not downloaded. For link context menus, open the link in a tab first.
